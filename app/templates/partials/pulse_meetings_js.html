{# Dialogs and behaviour for the Onboarding notes and Read AI panels. Included once on the client page. - [data-transcript-url] opens the transcript dialog at the cited passage - [data-confirm] asks first, then fires the element's hx-trigger="confirmed" request - [data-ob-open] switches the notes from reading to editing - [data-ob-cancel] reloads the notes panel, discarding the edit Native with showModal(), so focus is trapped and Escape closes it; the browser's own confirm() is never used. #}
