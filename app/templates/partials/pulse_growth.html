How this forecast works

The forecast continues the observed pace of Unstuck deals, using your average customer value and annual churn. It does not assume growth from other channels, predict payment timing, or add historical deals again to current revenue. Goals are ambitions, not guarantees. Lead requirements illustrate the scale needed if this channel alone filled the remaining gap; other growth activity can reduce that requirement.
