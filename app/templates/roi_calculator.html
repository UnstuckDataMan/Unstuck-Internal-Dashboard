How the projection works

Leads × conversion rate estimates new clients. A sale is booked when a whole deal is reached. Projects earn the fee once; retainers earn monthly fees from the sale month onwards.

Potential ROI = added revenue ÷ Unstuck fees over the selected period. A 1:1 ratio means projected revenue covers those fees; it is not a profit ROI percentage.

Sales and payment timing are illustrative. The model assumes immediate payment, no sales-cycle delay, a full first month and no existing clients. It excludes delivery costs, other expenses, taxes and inflation.

The separate churn scenario compounds monthly retention for retainers. For projects, it estimates fewer future opportunities, not losses on completed projects.
