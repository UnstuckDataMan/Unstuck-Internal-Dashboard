How these benchmarks are calculated

The scope: the latest three completed months by default, with an adjustable period. Unstuck’s own prospecting is included and identified as part of the sample. Company size describes the company targeted, not the agency’s client. Results are historical observations, not guaranteed volumes.

The source: the original Form Responses tab counts dated client records categorised Lead, even if the company name is blank. Interested and uncategorised records are excluded. Eight-column imports count dated, named lead rows. Exact duplicate rows are counted once. Blank or invalid required fields are excluded; unrecognised company sizes are excluded from size benchmarks. Dates use ISO or UK day/month/year format. These are recorded leads, not independently verified qualified leads.
