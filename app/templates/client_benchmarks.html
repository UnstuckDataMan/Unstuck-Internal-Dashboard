Benchmarks reflect historical client performance and are indicative rather than guaranteed. Results vary depending on targeting, industry, territory and campaign scope.

Methodology & sample sizes

Leads are pooled and divided by equivalent live client-months allocated to each segment. One client-month represents the client’s complete campaign portfolio, allocated across all eight headcount bands before grouping. Known zero-lead exposure is included. Partial months and the current month are excluded.

Allocation priority: unique prospects contacted, approved dated allocations, then estimated backend weights. Activity overrides use complete live months; otherwise the bounded interval between first and last observed qualifying leads is an activity estimate. Boundary months are excluded unless independently verified. Estimated inputs are labelled below.

Both Lead and Interested records count. ERI, Unstuck’s internal prospecting, test clients, invalid records and identifiable duplicates are excluded. Unknown company sizes and unknown activity/allocation are reported, not invented. Email identities are deduplicated per client; records without reliable identities are only exactly deduplicated.

Reporting window: latest six complete months for every segment, never before January 2025. Small businesses: 1–50 employees; Mid-market: 51–500; Enterprise: 501+. Companies with exactly 500 employees belong only to Mid-market. At least 3 clients and 6 equivalent months are required to publish a figure. Ranges show rounded sub-band rates when every sub-band meets that threshold; they are not confidence intervals. Otherwise the approximate pooled rate is shown.
